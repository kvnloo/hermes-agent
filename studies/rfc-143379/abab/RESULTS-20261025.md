## Decision and next falsifiers

A stateless `read` that computes its usage against the returned snapshot is
the minimum candidate worth comparing against adding a shared in-process
`RLock`. Both options must pass existing neighboring Hermes tests and
cross-process / external-edit / invalid-UTF8 / chain-document freshness cases.
Do not change original author-owned #141876 or #116673 without their review.

source-backed user questions, explicitly correct one, test model-initiated
