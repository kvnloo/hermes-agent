# upstream-contribution

Use when preparing or reviewing upstream changes, consolidating contributions, posting supplied bodies, or triaging follow-up replies.

## Rules

1. Match the exact repository's contribution culture, admission source, AI policy and named gates. Optimize maintainer time; distinguish user authorization, legal clearance, ownership, review capacity and technical readiness.
2. Keep planning, status and receipt-only requests within their read/research boundaries. Continue an explicitly authorized execution wave at its earliest missing gate; separate fork creation, pushes, upstream posts, merges and deployment permissions.
3. Recover canonical ledgers, surviving artifacts and live heads before restarting work. Assign one writer/publication owner per branch and PR; preserve other contributors' claimants, sibling work, explicit holds and cancelled-write clearance.
4. Inventory selected carriers and full relevant timelines before sweep writes. Reconcile paginated totals, files, reviews and CI; deduplicate by repository/carrier/mechanism and separate direct merges, salvage, equivalent adoption, research and unknown outcomes.
5. Pin fresh upstream and candidate SHAs in isolated stores; reproduce through real call sites. Require behavioral RED, candidate GREEN, property-removal controls, adjacent repo gates and independent review without weakening guards or assertion scope.
6. Bind receipts to source, base, environment, command, exit and counts. Separate setup failure, zero tests, skips, recovered receipts, overlays, local checks and native/browser execution. Use the platform-specific receipt references.
7. Preserve exact tested objects and required ancestry during delivery. Recheck remote heads/ownership, push explicit SHA refspecs atomically without tags and fast-forward by default; require explicit cleanup authorization and an exact-head lease for published rewrites.
8. Keep private paths, owner quotes, credentials and raw histories out of every outgoing object and public body. Preserve finder/design/test/implementation attribution and exact supplied source artifacts; package local-only work as a restored-and-tested durable bundle.
9. Post user-supplied ready bodies in the authorized turn without imposing authored-review reproduction gates. Preserve wording/fences, resolve exact destinations, reconcile complete duplicate checks and read back full text, author and URL. Honor superseding or cancelled batches.
10. For authored patches/reviews, use one narrow evidence-backed carrier unless a bounded wave is authorized. Preserve offers conditional on maintainer assent; verify draft/base/head/body after creation, inspect current-head CI and keep queue/admission holds explicit.
11. Read feedback/backoff state before posting. Require meaningful new evidence for outreach, honor the 72-hour pure-bump cooldown and repository posting bans, and report closed loops without thank-you spam. See references/live-feedback-watch.md.
12. Finish with exact local/remote heads, verified URLs, actual gate/CI outcomes, holds and next actions. Reconcile existing delivery separately from newly shipped work; retain durable evidence and earliest missing gates. See references/contribution-consolidation.md and references/thread-catch-up-and-claim-verification.md.
