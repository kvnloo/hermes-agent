# Replay-policy ecosystem research

## When to Use

Use for source-backed research on agent discovery-tree replay, policy-learning implementations, and creative-loop evaluation protocols.

## Procedure

1. Check project pages, author repositories, organization releases and commit history independently. A stale landing README may say code is unreleased after an organization publishes it. Pin the actual implementation SHA; read serializers, objectives, experiment scripts and exclusions.
2. Batch searches for title, paper identifier and domain. Persist raw responses, deduplicate/count programmatically, and record result caps/pagination. Reading lists are not implementations.
3. Separate citing papers, implementations, related workflows and changed forks. Verify creation/revision dates and default-branch comparisons; inaccessible or non-default heads remain unknown.
4. Read evaluation reports rather than badges. Record task/model/seeds, matched baselines, independent holdouts, judges, all-in costs, ablations, negative trials, raw artifacts and licenses. Separate synthetic tests, historical runs and prospective validation.
5. Exact replay requires recorded actions, complete prerequisites, prefix-visible information, frozen evaluator/context and compatible execution. Changed prompts, owner advice, models, scene state or resource contention require new executions. Do not invent counterfactual outcomes.
6. In creative loops separate runtime validity, reference fidelity and owner acceptance. Link blinded pairwise owner labels to immutable artifacts; split by scene lineage; calibrate judges out of sample; measure false acceptance, abstention and order bias.
7. Preserve failed attempts and charge policy development, critics, repairs, rendering, preparation and validation. Incumbent-inclusive selection cannot regress on its scoring history but gives no future guarantee.
8. Include simple scheduling baselines and paired prospective trials. Report baseline-success/candidate-failure cases despite positive averages. Null results are useful evidence.
9. Deliver a dated source table, bounded inventory, caveats and actionable methodology. Verify citations and counts. Do not execute untrusted code merely to research its claims.
