# launch-videos-library

Use when making, filing, finding or preparing publication of a launch film or feature-demo collection in the private launch-videos library.

## Rules

1. Keep film code in private `kvnloo/launch-videos` at `/mnt/zer0models/workspace/zer0/products/launch-videos`. Keep renders at their origin and inventory hashes in `film.json`. Read the reference films' contact sheets before authoring. For a feature wave, inventory issues, PRs, channel features and worktrees, then make one chaptered film plus cut-out feature clips only for runnable behavior. Mark bugs, code-less RFCs and unbuilt/uncommitted work as no-video with reasons.
2. Give plate lanes a BRIEF, BRIEF-COMMON, pinned source, separate app home/browser profile and free port. Inspect `/proc/*/cwd` and listening ports before use. Isolate an occupied checkout with `git worktree add --detach <film-dir> <sha>`. Serialize heavy work with `flock /tmp/<topic>-heavy.lock`; keep worker timeouts inside admission. Record every spawned process and leave other lanes' servers and browsers alone.
3. Keep plate-only lanes limited to capture and handback. Preserve pinned HEAD and a clean tree; leave product edits, commits, pushes, imports, posts and fframes film renders to their authorized owner. For reproduced defects, retain screenshots/DOM bounds and write `marks.json` with `plate: null` and `status: skipped-defect`, plus concrete NOTES. Repair and reshoot only under separate implementation scope.
4. Match the Omarchy bar: hook, title, full-screen section brands, sharp product-grade plates, CTA/credits and a calm CC0 music bed. Keep captions in a label band outside UI. Label synthetic data, scripted output, speed changes, concepts and credited recreations on screen. Preserve product restrictions, and treat replay test/performance numbers as fixture text rather than executed measurements.
5. Choose capture by product: real in-app film harness for T3, labelled native scrcpy for phones, deterministic BeginFrame for existing web products, and pure `window.seek(t)` scenes for other authored UI. Use `references/rendered-ui-scenes.md` for seek-safe kits and still-first review. Downscale high-resolution sources to 1080p yuv444 editing plates before fframes to bound renderer memory.
6. Capture immutable deployed snapshots with BeginFrame, monotonic real-clock `frameTimeTicks` plus virtual offsets and the tested launch flags. Preflight every interaction at production viewport/scale, warm server-backed content before pausing time, and pump frames until bounded input ACKs settle. Close persistent browsers with `Browser.close` and awaited exit so pairing persists. Keep keychain bypass flags inside disposable capture profiles and redact pairing fragments.
7. Use `references/replay-agent-fixtures.md` for genuine UI states without real model calls. Keep real ACP orchestration, explicit specific-before-generic prompt routing and quiet fallback. Smoke-test embedded instructions through actual session/new and session/prompt. Verify settings after reload against effective backend behavior. Resume stateful takes only after checking side effects and disclose editorial joins.
8. For Hermes Desktop films, follow `references/hermes-desktop-deterministic-capture.md`: serve the branch renderer through a sandbox gateway, use unique owned units, mint a one-time ticket per take without logging it, and frame-lock scripted streaming with ticks/reset. Fit boards on both axes and inspect settled density/loading. Reserve Electron/Xvfb for Electron-only verification, not final film footage.
9. Prepare synthetic servers and assets before taking a phone lock. Revalidate transport/device and save foreground, wallpaper and environment state, then capture the shortest complete take first. Choose taps from completed screenshots and validate custom fences separately from native rendering. Supervise every pipeline component, restore and verify saved state before releasing the lock, and report transport-blocked restoration as incomplete cleanup.
10. Build picture and sound from one beat-aligned timeline. Use `dynamic_media/`, `MediaDirectory::read_folder` and CombinedMediaProvider for fframes 1.3 video/audio. Copy template Cargo.lock, define scenes whose durations sum to the film, and make plates outlast their visible transitions. Rebuild after timeline changes and retire stale `out/parts`. Probe frames/strips and inspect contact sheets before full renders; cut chapters plus CTA from the final film without a second GPU render.
11. Re-run the full handback gate over every plate after gate changes. Deliver `plate-1080.mp4`, `plate-2160.mp4`, 12-frame `contact.png`, encoded `payoff-1080.png`, `marks.json` and NOTES. Independently verify exact dimensions, pixel format, fps, frame count and seconds, fully decode the 1080p master, and vision-check encoded contact/payoff plus source readability. Write hash/receipt inventory to DELIVERY.json before teardown and handback.
12. Bound decoder, implicit filter and encoder threads separately. Use resumable PNGs and verified concat-safe segments under pressure, preserve `rejected-partial-<res>.mp4`, and accept only complete exact-count masters. Share the warm target directory with `-j 1`, preserve resource-termination receipts and retry only invalid outputs. Stop owned servers including stdin feeders, await browser exit and verify closed ports before declaring handback complete.
13. Before updating the library, move bulk PNGs and rejected cuts out of source. Update registry.json with `indent=1`, then run `python3 tools/collect.py <id>`, `python3 tools/build_timeline.py` and `python3 tools/sync_fframestudio.py`. Restore hand-added film.json keys after collection. In the fframestudio checkout, run `python3 tools/import_projects.py <id>`. Update launch-videos docs/timeline.png. Keep non-fframes import support and publication authority explicit.
14. Preserve yuv444p masters and make separate browser previews as 1080p60 H.264 yuv420p with `-movflags +faststart`, checking frame counts and client file delivery. Embed inline T3 copies with image-style Markdown pointing to the actual absolute preview path, and label plates versus finished edits. Keep PR media off code branches on `media/<topic>` and verify audio/contact sheets before upload. Use user-attachments for GitHub inline playback and wait for approval before posting wave material.
